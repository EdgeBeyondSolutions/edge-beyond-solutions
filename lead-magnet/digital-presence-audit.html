How to use this audit

This isn't a reading exercise — it's a self-diagnostic. Go through all five pillars below and check off every statement that's honestly true for your business right now. At the end, you'll add up your checkmarks and see exactly where you stand.

Each pillar covers a single, specific thing a prospect evaluates — often without realizing it — in the sixty seconds between finding your business and deciding whether to contact you. None of these require a developer, an agency, or a budget to check. They require five honest minutes.

The fifth pillar is different from what you'll find in a typical marketing checklist. It's the one area where most small businesses lose the most revenue, and the one almost nobody audits for themselves.

A note on honesty

This only works if you're accurate. Checking a box because it's "mostly true" or "true for an old version of the site" defeats the purpose. Grade yourself the way a stranger searching for your business tonight would.

Pillar one
Website — First Impression & Conversion

Your website is the only employee that works 24 hours a day — but only if it actually does the job. Most small-business sites lose prospects in the first few seconds, long before anyone reads a word of copy.

Your site loads in under 3 seconds on mobile data, not just office Wi-Fi.
A first-time visitor can tell what you do and where you're located without scrolling.
There's a way to reach you that isn't just a phone number — a form, chat, or booking link.
The design doesn't look like it was built more than 3-4 years ago.
Someone on a phone can tap to call you in a single tap — no pinching, no zooming.
Cost of an unchecked box

Each one is a specific, nameable reason a prospect leaves your site and calls the next result on the page instead — one that never shows up in any report, because they never told you why.

Pillar two
Google Business Profile — Local Trust Signal

For almost every local search, your Google Business Profile is seen before your website — sometimes instead of it. It's frequently the single biggest factor in whether someone picks up the phone.

Your Google Business Profile is claimed and verified under your control.
You have more than 10 reviews.
Your average rating is 4.5 stars or higher.
You've responded to your most recent reviews — the good ones and the bad ones.
You've added new photos in the last 90 days.
Cost of an unchecked box

An unclaimed or stale profile doesn't just underperform — it actively signals "this business may not be operating anymore" to someone comparing you against three competitors in the same search.

03
Pillar three
Social Proof — Reviews & Activity

Five-star reviews are one of the most valuable assets a small business owns — but only if they're actually seen at the moment someone is deciding whether to trust you.

Your best reviews are visible somewhere other than Google — your site, social, or both.
At least one of your social profiles has posted in the last 30 days.
You have at least one video or photo testimonial from a real customer, not just text.
You know which single platform your actual customers use most — and you're active there.
Cost of an unchecked box

A five-star reputation that lives only on Google, with no recent activity anywhere else, reads as inactive to a prospect scrolling your social channels before they call.

Pillar four
Listings Consistency — Name, Address, Phone

Google, Yelp, Apple Maps, Bing and industry directories each hold their own copy of your business information. When they disagree with each other, it creates friction — and sometimes sends people to the wrong number entirely.

Your business name, address and phone number match exactly across Google, Yelp, Facebook and Apple Maps.
You've personally checked your listings in the last 12 months.
An old, closed, or incorrect listing isn't still showing up when someone searches your name.
Your website URL is correct everywhere it appears.
Cost of an unchecked box

A mismatched phone number or a duplicate listing doesn't just look unprofessional — search engines read inconsistency as a trust signal against you, quietly ranking consistent competitors above you.

05
Pillar five · The one most checklists skip
Response Speed & AI Readiness

A perfect website and a five-star profile still lose the sale if nobody — or nothing — responds fast enough. Today's customer expects an answer in minutes, not by the next business day, at any hour they happen to be looking.

A new website inquiry gets a response within 5 minutes, at any hour — not just during business hours.
A missed call automatically triggers a follow-up text, instead of simply going unanswered.
A prospect can book or schedule without needing to call during business hours.
You can say, with a real number, how many leads you lost last month simply because nobody answered in time.
Cost of an unchecked box

This is the pillar with the most direct line to lost revenue — and the one an AI agent or automation fixes fastest, because it's not a content problem, it's a response-time problem.
